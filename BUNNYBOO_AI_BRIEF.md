# Bunnyboo — brand and website brief

Use this as the persistent project brief when prompting an AI website builder such as OpenPage. It is a brand specification, not model training.

## Brand
- Name: Bunnyboo
- Product to launch with: bunny-ear headband
- Audience: gift buyers and people shopping for playful accessories; use childlike warmth without assuming the buyer or wearer is a specific age.
- Positioning: premium-cute, storybook, playful, giftable, polished rather than cheap-looking.
- Brand voice: warm, sweet, short, specific, joyful; never pushy or manipulative.
- Core message: “Small things. Big magic.”

## Visual direction
- Palette: cream #FFF8F3, blush #F7DFE4, rose #BD6E83, deep rose #8F465C, peach #F8E7D9, butter yellow #FFF0BD, mint #DCEBE0, ink #33242A.
- Type: editorial serif headlines, readable sans-serif body text.
- Shape language: soft corners, gentle curves, small hearts, flowers, ribbons, storybook-like product staging.
- Balance: premium editorial whitespace + playful accents. Avoid an overloaded toy-store look.
- Mobile first: easy thumb reach, readable type, clear product CTAs, no horizontal overflow.
- Photography: use original photos of the inspected physical sample for the live store. Clearly label illustrations/mockups as concepts until then.

## Conversion goals
1. Show the product and the reason to care within the first screen.
2. Use one primary action consistently: “Meet the bunny ears” / “Add to bag.”
3. Build desire with gift, celebration, dress-up, and memory-making use cases.
4. Answer practical questions near the product: color, material, fit, package contents, price, shipping, returns.
5. Keep navigation short; include FAQs and visible contact/support access.
6. Keep product choice and quantity controls simple.
7. Support authentic product photos, verified details, an accessible cart, payment, inventory, shipping, and order confirmation before accepting real orders.
8. Measure real outcomes when analytics is later configured: product CTA clicks, add-to-cart rate, checkout start, completed orders, refunds, and conversion by traffic source/creative.

## Truth and trust rules
- Never invent customer reviews, star ratings, sales counts, scarcity, certifications, delivery dates, discounts, or product safety claims.
- Keep price, color availability, material, dimensions, age guidance, delivery, and returns marked “to be confirmed” until verified.
- Do not claim checkout, stock, email signup, or payment works unless the integration is connected and tested.
- Publish clear shipping, cancellation, privacy, and return policies before launch.
- Use only licensed or owned product images and brand assets.

## Future AI-builder prompt
“Create and refine Bunnyboo, a premium-cute, storybook-inspired storefront for a bunny-ear headband. Use the brand palette and voice in this brief. Prioritize mobile usability and purchase conversion without manipulative dark patterns. Above the fold, communicate the product’s emotional benefit, show a clear primary product CTA, and use a polished hero composition. Include product details, gift use cases, original-photo placeholders clearly labeled as placeholders, customer FAQs, accessible navigation, shipping/returns/policy destinations, and a restrained newsletter signup. Do not create fake reviews, fake scarcity, made-up product specifications, prices, claims, or delivery estimates. Keep the layout fast, accessible, responsive, and straightforward to edit. Separate mockup behavior from real commerce integrations, and document any unconnected features.”
